How we would pay for these

The points and miles angle on resident rates

Kamaʻāina rates are cash rates

That is good news for us. You earn on the spend instead of burning points, and a 30 percent resident rate often beats the same night booked with points.

Book direct with the property

Third party sites usually will not give you points or elite night credit, and most kamaʻāina rates require you to book on the hotel's own site anyway.

Confirm earning before you pay

Some discounted resident rates are excluded from loyalty earning and elite credit. Ask at booking rather than after checkout.

Bring your Hawaiʻi ID

Nearly every offer here requires proof of residency at check in or at the door. A driver license or state ID is the standard.

How the value score works

The score is ours. We read each offer and weight four things: the size of the discount on the main rate or ticket, any fees that get waived or cut such as parking and resort fees, the extras thrown in such as breakfast, upgrades or late check out, and the dollar value the business states for those extras. An offer that says "up to" a number is discounted slightly, because in practice you rarely get the top of that range.

A high score means the offer is generous relative to the others on this board. It does not mean the rate itself is cheap, and it cannot tell you what the property will actually quote you on your dates. Use it to decide what is worth a look, then check the real numbers.
